# Future Me

A money game about the person you are becoming. Budget your first salary, survive life, and read letters from Future You. English and Arabic (right-to-left), AED or USD.

Made for **Zoud GameLab — "Make FinLit Fun"**.

- Play in the browser: https://goat99zyx.github.io/future-me/ (also on itch.io: https://goat99zyx.itch.io/future-me)
- Concept document (PDF): [FutureMe-Concept-Doc.pdf](FutureMe-Concept-Doc.pdf)
- Game budget (PDF): [FutureMe-Budget.pdf](FutureMe-Budget.pdf)
- Gameplay video (2 min, captured from the game): [futureme-gameplay.mp4](futureme-gameplay.mp4)

The whole game is one file, `index.html`. It works offline and collects no data.

**AI disclosure:** the code, the English and Arabic text and the simple vector art were made with AI assistance (Claude). Sounds are generated in the browser. No third-party assets.

The game simplifies real finance for learning. It is not financial advice.
